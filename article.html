VM Mail & Invoice Studio — Add-ons & Updates

Extend your Studio with separately purchased email and invoice template packs.

Template Pack v1.0

Four matching email and invoice designs: Monochrome Professional, Blue Professional, Minimal Signature, and Pink & Teal.

Latest pack version: 1.0. Minimum Studio core: 1.0.1.

Four designs included

Each design includes an HTML email layout and an invoice layout for use through Studio:

Monochrome Professional

a restrained monochrome style for business correspondence.

Blue Professional

blue headings and wave artwork for a distinctive, coordinated appearance.

Minimal Signature

a simple, understated layout that keeps attention on the order details. No handwritten or digital signature is included.

Pink & Teal

a colourful design with decorative corner artwork.

The pack includes compact invoice details, distinct item-table styling, email wrappers and the decorative artwork used by the designs. Design colour palettes are fixed.

How to install or update a pack

  1. Download the correct edition from our shop.
  2. Install the ZIP using Joomla’s extension installer. For an update, install over the existing pack.
  3. Enable the design plugin and select its design inside Studio.

Studio 1.0.2 displays available packs and version notices in Add-ons & News. Purchases and downloads are handled on our website.
